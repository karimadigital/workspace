## Writing Rules (Non-Negotiable)

From the voice guide. No exceptions.

- **Never use em dashes.** Period or rewrite.
- **No repetitive parallel structure.** "I code when I'm tired. I code when I'm scared." = AI tell.
- **No staccato sentence chains.** Let sentences breathe.
- **No AI phrases.** "Delve," "leverage," "game-changer," "excited to share," "amazing journey," "so grateful," "the real unlock."
- **No "most people" framing.** Karima doesn't position herself above her audience.
- **No fabricated stats.** Proof points must be verified.
- **No claims about time that hasn't passed.**
- **Sentence case.** Lowercase is seasoning, not the dish.
- **Don't sanitize emotion.** If it's a hard moment, let it feel hard.

**Posture test:** Does this sound like Karima sharing something she found, or telling the reader they're behind? If the second, rewrite.

## Final Check Before Sending

1. Does this sound like Karima or like AI wrote it?
2. Does this sound like sharing something found, or telling them they're behind?
3. Em dashes? Remove.
4. "Most people" anywhere? Rewrite.
5. Talking down to audience? Rewrite.
6. Three parallel sentences in a row? Rewrite.
7. Too polished? Make it rawer.
8. Could serve two goals instead of one? Push harder.
9. When in doubt: warmer, more direct, more real.
