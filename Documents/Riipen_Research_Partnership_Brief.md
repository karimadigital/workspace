# Riipen Research Partnership Brief
## Crash Out Diary - AI Wellness Games for Gen Z Women

---

## About Crash Out Diary

**Crash Out Diary** is a wellness tech startup building AI-powered games designed to help Gen Z women (ages 13-25) manage stress, emotional regulation, and mental health through playful, gamified experiences.

**Our Mission:** Create technology that meets young women where they are - on their phones, in their feelings - and gives them tools to "crash out" (release emotions) in healthy ways.

---

## The Problem We're Solving

### The Mental Health Crisis is Real:
- **63% of Gen Z** report feeling lonely on a weekly basis (Eventbrite Social Study 2026)
- **73% of Gen Z women** say they're more likely to attend wellness-focused events than any other generation
- AI chatbots are becoming the **first place young people turn** for mental health support - but current solutions ignore psychological safety risks (World Psychiatry, 2026)
- The gap between needing help and getting it is growing - waitlists, cost, stigma all create barriers

### Why Games?
- Gen Z spends 7+ hours daily on screens
- Games reduce stigma - it's "playing" not "therapy"
- Gamification increases engagement and habit formation
- Wellness through play meets them where they already are

## Research Partnership Opportunity

### What We're Looking For:
University students (undergraduate or graduate) in:
- **Psychology / Clinical Psychology** - Validate therapeutic frameworks
- **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** - UX research on AI wellness interactions  
- **Game Design / Gamification** - Evidence-based game mechanics for emotional regulation
- **Public Health** - Measure wellness outcomes in Gen Z populations
- **AI/ML** - Responsible AI development for mental health applications

### Project Ideas:

**1. AI Companion Safety Research**
- How do we build AI wellness companions that DON'T increase psychosis risk?
- Literature review + design guidelines
- Reference: Wiley study on AI chatbots and psychological safety

**2. Gamified Emotional Regulation Study**
- Test effectiveness of game-based interventions vs. traditional apps
- Pre/post measurement of stress, anxiety, emotional awareness
- Target: Gen Z women 18-25

**3. "Crash Out" Behavior Analysis**
- Qualitative research on how Gen Z women currently "crash out" (release emotions)
- What healthy vs. unhealthy patterns exist?
- How can games redirect unhealthy patterns?

**4. AI Personalization Ethics**
- What level of AI personalization is helpful vs. harmful for mental health?
- Privacy considerations for vulnerable populations
- Guidelines for responsible wellness AI
