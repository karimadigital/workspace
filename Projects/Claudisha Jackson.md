# Claudisha Jackson — One-Page Brief

**What it is:** Karima's AI marketing team. Six AI "employees" that share one brain, so everything they make sounds like her. It runs three ways: Claude Code, Telegram (coming), and a dashboard called The Office.

**Why it exists (two jobs at once):**

1. It runs Karima's own marketing day to day (she's "client zero," her own case study).
2. The same system is the done-for-you deliverable she sells as a **VIP Day / VIP Build** ($2,500–$5,000 per employee).

**The shape: one brain, six workers, three doors.**

**One brain** — a shared source of truth every employee reads before writing: voice DNA (no em dashes, banned phrases, Threads posts under \~500 characters), hook library, format specs, ICP, funnel, brand pillars, persona. Lives as files on disk (the working copy) and mirrored in Notion (the showable copy). They stay in sync.

**The six employees:**

- **Margo** — Content Strategist. Weekly Monday strategy, campaign briefs, fixes the sales/landing pages.
- **Penn** — Content Writer. Net-new posts, captions, emails, threads.
- **Callie** — Content Planner. Turns briefs into a 2-week calendar.
- **Remi** — Repurposer. Turns one piece of content into many (from reels, docs, call notes, long-form).
- **Tia** — Trends Scout. Surfaces trends and competitor moves.
- **Ada** — Data Insights. Reports what hit and why; always ends with what to do next.

**Claudisha** is the front desk. Karima talks to her and she routes to the right employee. Karima can also talk to any employee directly by name (`/margo`, `/ada`, etc.).

**The three doors (same team, three ways in):**

1. **Claude Code** — where the heavy work happens. Live now.
2. **The Office** — a local dashboard/workspace (ocean-sunset theme, light mode) where employees file their work and Karima reviews it with real Approve / Edit / Send-back buttons. Tabs by function: Overview, Content Bank, Analytics, What's Trending, Content Calendar. Live now.
3. **Telegram** — pocket assistant for on-the-go requests. Coming next.

**How it runs on its own:** Daily loops handle a morning briefing (what ran, what broke, what's ready), Instagram number refreshes, and repurpose candidates. Every loop follows one rule: schedule + do the work + report back.

**The rule that matters most:** every piece of writing starts by reading the brain, and every call-to-action comes from the funnel. Nothing is written in a generic voice, and nothing is invented.
