# Brain-dump prompt

Paste this into your other system (the one that already knows you — Notion Vault / your Claude Project). It outputs ten labeled blocks. Each block maps to one file in `brain/`. Hand the output back to Aura and she drops each block into its file, and the Opportunities system is armed.

Rules baked in: real figures only, mark anything unverified, no invented clients or stats, and your voice law (no em dashes, no banned phrases). Property management is background, not your #1 vertical.

---

You are helping me arm an AI agent that hunts paid opportunities (speaking, teaching, training contracts, partnerships, awards, and warm-adjacent "shots" at big targets) and drafts applications in my voice. It only wins if the facts you give it are real and specific. Pull from everything you know about me and my business.

Output EXACTLY ten blocks in this order, each under its filename heading, each in a fenced code block so I can copy it cleanly. Follow every rule.

GLOBAL RULES
- Use real, specific numbers. If a figure is not confirmed, write it and tag it "(unverified)". Never invent a client, stat, testimonial, or credential.
- Voice law for anything written in my voice: no em dashes (use a period or rewrite), no "delve / leverage / game-changer / excited to share / the real unlock / dive into," no "most people" framing, no talking down.
- Property management is where I have the most background, but it is NOT my #1 vertical. Do not rank it first.
- If you genuinely don't know a section, write "NEEDS KARIMA" and list the 2-3 questions I'd need to answer. Don't pad.

=== 1. proof-bank.md ===
Every safe-to-claim fact the agent may cite in an application. One bullet each: the claim + the real number + verified/unverified. Include audience sizes (followers, list, community, sessions/views), revenue milestones, client outcomes with figures, named or anonymized clients, testimonials, press, credentials, education, and anything I've built with its measurable result.

=== 2. offer-ladder.md ===
My offers and prices, top to bottom (high-ticket program, VIP day, workshops, training day rate, speaking fee range, paid community tier). For each: what it is, the price, and who it's for. Then my fee floor, what I'll do free strategically and why, and a one-line note per opportunity type on how it feeds revenue (e.g. "a podcast only counts if it feeds the book or the high-ticket offer").

=== 3. money-window.md ===
What "money in the next 30 days" means to me (booked, invoiced, or cash in hand), my real availability constraints over a 30-day window, and my payment-terms preferences (deposit up front, net-30, etc.).

=== 4. network.md ===
My warm relationship graph. Past and current clients, people who've expressed interest, dormant leads, and anyone who runs or connects to rooms I want (summits, universities, associations, podcasts). Name names where you can, with a word on the connection. This powers the "shot" tier.

=== 5. affiliations.md ===
Schools and alma maters (with faculty/alum ties), communities and memberships I'm in, and past collaborators, orgs, and programs. These are the bridges that turn a cold target into a warm-adjacent shot.

=== 6. criteria.md ===
My fit filter. Verticals that actually contain buyers who can afford my high-ticket offer, ranked (property management is NOT first). Location weighting (in-person region vs virtual anywhere). Hard red lines (pay-to-play, vanity awards, exposure-only unless strategic). Which of the five types are ON (Stages, Classrooms, Contracts, Partnerships, Recognition). Whether the Shot tier is ON and the size threshold that qualifies it (e.g. university teaching, marquee keynote, named program only). Standing territory to always sweep (e.g. AI / founder / creator rooms). My Human Design type if relevant (Manifestor, 4/1) and what that means for initiating.

=== 7. voice-dna.md ===
The full rules for how I sound: my modes, sentence rhythm, the hard bans (no em dashes, the banned-phrase list, no "most people," no talking down), and the posture test (am I sharing something I found, or telling the reader they're behind). Pull from my existing voice guide if you have it.

=== 8. positioning.md ===
My differentiator in one or two lines. What makes a "why it fits" specific instead of filler. Include "that Claude girl," practitioner-led, and the one-sentence version of what I do and for whom.

=== 9. bio.md ===
A short bio (2-3 sentences, application-ready) and a long bio (one paragraph). Plus one or two ready-to-deliver talks: title, one-paragraph abstract, three takeaways each. Organizers book packaged talks fast.

=== 10. history.md ===
What I've applied to, won, and lost, even a rough list, so the agent can learn my real conversion instead of guessing. Anything I've pitched, spoken at, taught, or been rejected from counts.
