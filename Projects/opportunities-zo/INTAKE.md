# First-run interview (the intake)

This is what the system asks a new user on setup. It runs when the dashboard loads with empty Criteria. The answers become the files in `brain/` that every agent reads on each run. This is the only personal content in the system, and it's the depersonalization layer: ship blank, interview, and it's theirs.

**Why the interview matters more than the search.** The Finder can search brilliantly and still land nothing if the inputs below are thin. The proof bank, the offer ladder, and the warm network are what turn a found door into money. Search is the easy 20 percent. These are the 80 percent, and only the user can supply them.

Each section maps to one `brain/` file. Fill them and the system is armed.

---

## 1. Proof bank → `brain/proof-bank.md` (non-negotiable)

The Drafter cannot win anything without concrete, safe-to-claim facts. Thin proof = generic applications = losing.

- Audience numbers (followers, list size, community size, sessions/views).
- Revenue milestones and client outcomes **with real figures**.
- Named or anonymized clients, testimonials, press, credentials, education.
- Anything you've built and its measurable result.

**Every entry marked verified**, so nothing gets fabricated under pressure. This is the single biggest lever on whether a draft lands.

## 2. Offer ladder → `brain/offer-ladder.md` (the money link)

An opportunity is only "fruitful" if it converts to an offer. The agent scores by money produced, not face comp.

- Your offers and prices (high-ticket program, VIP day, workshops, training day rate, speaking fee range, paid community tier).
- Your fee floor, and what you'll do free *strategically* and why.
- How each opportunity type feeds revenue (a podcast only counts if it feeds the book or the high-ticket offer).

## 3. Speed-to-cash → `brain/money-window.md` (makes it a 30-day agent)

- What "money in 30 days" means to you: booked, invoiced, or cash in hand.
- Your real availability for the next 30 days (pulled from calendar), so it never surfaces something you can't deliver in the window.
- Payment-terms preferences (deposit up front, net-30, etc.).

## 4. Warm network → `brain/network.md` + `brain/affiliations.md` (the fastest door)

The highest-leverage input is not a search term, it's your relationship graph. It's also what powers the **Shot** tier — no network means no findable bridge, so the system correctly won't shoot a shot.

**`brain/network.md`:**
- Past and current clients.
- People who've expressed interest; dormant leads.
- Anyone who runs, or connects to, the rooms you want.

**`brain/affiliations.md`:**
- Schools, alma maters, faculty/alum ties.
- Communities and memberships you're part of.
- Past collaborators, orgs, programs.

The agent cross-references every found opportunity against these, and turns a "cold" university into a warm-adjacent Shot it can actually justify — with a named bridge.

## 5. Fit filter → `brain/criteria.md`

- Which verticals actually contain buyers who can afford the high-ticket offer.
- Location weighting (in-person region vs virtual anywhere).
- Hard red lines: pay-to-play, vanity awards, exposure-only unless strategic.
- The five opportunity types: which are ON. Whether the **Shot** tier is ON, and the size threshold that qualifies (e.g. university teaching, marquee keynote, named program only).
- Standing territory to always sweep (e.g. AI/founder/creator rooms).

## 6. Voice + positioning → `brain/voice-dna.md`, `brain/positioning.md`, `brain/bio.md`

- Voice DNA: the rules for how you sound. Hard bans (no em dashes, banned phrases, no talking down to the reader). The Drafter treats this as law.
- Positioning: your differentiator, in one or two lines. What makes "why it fits" specific instead of filler.
- Bio: a short and a long version, ready to paste into an application.
- A ready-to-deliver talk or two: title, abstract, three takeaways. Organizers book packaged talks fast — a ready talk raises close rate more than twenty more listings.

## 7. Feedback seed → `brain/history.md`

- What you've applied to, won, and lost — even a rough list.
- Lets the agent learn your real conversion instead of guessing.

---

## Human Design note (why the Shot tier exists)

If the user is a Manifestor, initiating is on-strategy. For a 4/1 line specifically, success comes through the network (4) from a foundation of authority (1). So approaching first is fine; approaching *cold* is not. The interview should capture design type if the user knows it, because it justifies whether the Shot tier is on at all and how aggressively to weight the network. If they don't know it or don't work this way, the Shot tier can stay off and the system runs on the five public-door hunters alone.

---

## What "done" looks like

Seven files in `brain/`, all filled. Criteria record populated. The empty-Criteria interview no longer fires. The Finder now has topics, industries, a fee floor, a network to bridge from, and a proof bank to draft from. The system is personal-armed but still ships blank for the next person.
