Karima Williams is an AI consultant and educator whose work has been featured on Oprah's podcast, Essence, Business Insider, and Afrotech. A founder herself, she combined AI with her non-technical background to build two viral apps: an emotional wellness app that scaled to more than 191,000 users across 187 countries with zero ad spend, and a local voter guide that reached 21,000 voters in less than 23 days. Today she runs the AI Employee Lab, where she builds AI employees for founders and CEOs of growing businesses, taking the repetitive work off their plates so they can focus on what actually grows revenue. She's the author of The AI Employee Handbook: Hire Your First AI Employee with Claude and leads the AI Power Lab, a community of 1,200+ builders with 22 live classes delivered. A Howard University graduate, she was a Web3 operator before AI, managing a $720K grants program at ConsenSys and helping close a $1M crowdsale in 36 hours.
