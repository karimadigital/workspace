## Red lines (auto-reject)

- **Any fee to apply = auto-reject. Hard line, no exceptions.** If it costs money to submit the application itself, it never reaches the board. This covers grants with application fees and award/recognition entry fees. Do not surface it and let Karima judge. Drop it. (Set by Karima, Aug 24 2026.)
  - **Professional membership is NOT an application fee.** Chamber of Commerce, trade association, and similar org memberships are ordinary business costs with their own standalone value, and Karima judges those herself. Keep those opportunities, and state the membership requirement plainly in `costToApply`. The line is: paying to be *considered* is out, paying to *belong to a business organization* is her call. (Set by Karima, Aug 24 2026.)
- No pay-to-play. No vanity awards. No exposure-only (unless it's strategic authority). No cold outreach.
- Not product / e-commerce audiences. Not pre-revenue or year-one rooms.
