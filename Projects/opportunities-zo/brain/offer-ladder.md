## Speaking fee: agent proposes, Karima approves

Karima has no fixed speaking fee or floor on file, and that is intentional for now. **When an opportunity needs a number, the agent suggests what to pitch instead of pulling a stored rate.** Rules:

- Propose a **range**, not a single figure, based on what the run can see: audience size, the organizer's budget signals (corporate vs association vs community vs university), format (keynote vs workshop vs panel), whether travel is covered, and comparable market rates for an AI/marketing practitioner speaker.
- Present it as **2–3 options** (e.g. hold-firm / market / stretch) with one line of reasoning each, so Karima approves or adjusts. Never state a fee as committed.
- Anchor to the ladder, not the air: a paid talk should be framed to feed the **$997 Audit** funnel, so a lower speaking fee can still be worth it if the room contains buyers. Say so when it does.
- If a form demands a hard number and the agent can't justify one from the run, **flag it "needs your eyes"** rather than inventing a rate.
- The free **Masterclass "Hire Your First AI Employee"** remains the strategic free lead event. Free is a funnel move, never a default.
